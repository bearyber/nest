Nest icon drafts

Drafts for the Nest by INTRVL app icon. Options 5 to 7 blend option 3's channel-striped folder with option 4's N; the first round follows for reference. Each is shown at 256px, then at 64, 32 and 16px, on a light and a dark desktop. The 16 and 32px sizes are what Windows shows in the taskbar and Explorer.

Mix of 3 + 4

Option 5

Graded tab end: the off-white N from option 4, with its raised right end split into red, green and blue bands.

Option 6

N on the folder: option 3's folder with the channel-striped tab, and a charcoal N set on the folder front.

Option 7

Three-channel N: each stroke of the N carries one muted channel tone, so the letter is the red, green and blue.

First round

Option 1

Nested folders: three folders set one inside the next, with the innermost job folder in amber.

Option 2

Bowl and egg: two concentric arcs cradle a single cobalt egg on warm off-white.

Option 3

Graded folder: a plain folder whose tab carries red, green and blue tones, the colorist's three channels.

Option 4

Tab monogram: an N drawn as a folder-tab profile turned on its side, with the raised tab end in blue.

On a high-density screen the 16 and 32px previews are drawn with twice the pixels, so they look slightly sharper than on a standard 1080p monitor.
